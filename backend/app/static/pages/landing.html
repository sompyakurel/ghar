What's inside

  • 22 lesson decks — alphabet, numbers, colors, food, family, animals, Dashain & Tihar, and for teens, the slang their cousins actually use
  • Three age modes — Seedling (5–8), Explorer (9–13), Rooted (14–20)
  • Every word spoken aloud, with letter-tracing and writing practice
  • Quizzes & games — Quiz Blitz, Balloon Pop, and Bagh-Chal, the classic Nepali board game
  • Real-world missions to do with family, a pet dog to earn, and a cozy virtual home to decorate
  • Family leaderboards — cousins compete with a nickname and a group code
