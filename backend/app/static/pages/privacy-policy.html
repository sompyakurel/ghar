Ghar: Learn Nepali — Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

The short version: Ghar is a language-learning app made for children and their families. It has no user accounts, no advertising, and no tracking. Almost everything the app does happens entirely on your device. The single exception is an optional leaderboard, described below, which sends a nickname and scores — nothing else — to our server.

Information that stays on your device

Learning progress, XP, streaks, settings, and decorations are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

Voice recordings never leave the device. Ghar includes features that use the microphone — recording a "teach-back" for a family member to check, and pronunciation practice where a child records their voice and compares it with the model. These recordings are stored only on the device (practice recordings are temporary and are deleted when you leave the screen). They are never uploaded, transcribed, or shared.

The one exception: leaderboards (optional)

If — and only if — a user chooses to join a leaderboard group, the app sends the following to our server so the group can see its standings:

  • The nickname the user picks for themselves
  • The code of the group they created or joined
  • Their scores: total XP, best quiz streak, total correct quiz answers, and missions completed

That is all. The leaderboard does not collect a real name, email address, device identifier, location, or any other information. Nicknames and scores are visible to other members of the same group. Leaderboard data is transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and stored on our own server.

Removing leaderboard data: Tap "Delete my scores" in the app at any time to permanently delete a player's scores from the server. Groups with no activity for 30 days are automatically deleted, along with their scores.

Purchases

The optional "Ghar Premium" in-app purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We never receive or store payment card details. Family Sharing of the purchase is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy.

Third parties

Ghar contains no advertising, no analytics services, and no third-party tracking of any kind. We do not sell, rent, or share information with third parties for marketing. The only external service involved is the hosting for our own leaderboard server (Fly.io), which stores the leaderboard data described above.

Children's privacy

Ghar is designed for children, including children under 13. Because the app has no accounts and collects nothing beyond the optional leaderboard nickname and scores described above, there is no personal information for us to use for any other purpose. A nickname does not have to be a real name, and we encourage children to use a fun nickname rather than their real name. Parents or guardians can delete a child's leaderboard scores at any time using "Delete my scores" in the app, or by contacting us at the email below and asking us to remove them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and change the effective date above. Material changes affecting the leaderboard will also be noted in the app's update notes.
