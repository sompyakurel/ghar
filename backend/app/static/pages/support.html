I bought Ghar Premium, but the content is still locked

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID that made the purchase, then: open Ghar → tap any locked lesson → on the paywall, tap Restore Purchases. Your unlock will come back. This also works when you move to a new phone.

How does Family Sharing work?

Ghar Premium is a one-time purchase with Family Sharing included. If the person who bought it has Family Sharing turned on, family members can open Ghar on their own devices (signed in with their own Apple IDs in the same family group) and use Restore Purchases on the paywall to activate the unlock — no second payment.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. It's a single one-time payment. There is no recurring charge, ever.

How do I delete leaderboard scores?

Open the Leaderboard tab in the app and tap Delete my scores. This permanently removes that player's scores from our server. To leave the leaderboard entirely, you can also simply stop using it — groups with no activity for 30 days are deleted automatically.

The microphone doesn't work for speaking practice or missions

Ghar needs microphone permission for those features. Go to Settings → Ghar → Microphone and turn it on. Recordings are stored only on the device and are never uploaded.

Does Ghar work offline?

Yes. All lessons, audio, and games are inside the app and work without an internet connection. Only the optional leaderboard needs the internet, to sync scores with your group.

Does my child need an account?

No. Ghar has no accounts and no sign-in. If your child joins a leaderboard, they only pick a nickname — we encourage a fun nickname rather than a real name.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy for exactly what data the app does and doesn't collect. The short version: no ads, no tracking, no accounts, and voice recordings never leave the device.
